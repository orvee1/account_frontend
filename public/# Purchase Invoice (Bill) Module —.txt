## 1. Multi-Unit of Measure (Multi-UOM) — Same as Sales

Purchase follows the exact same Multi-UOM logic as sales:

- Every product has a **base unit** (conversion_factor = 1) — stock always stored in base unit
- At purchase entry time, user selects two UOMs independently:
  1. **Purchase UOM** — the unit in which quantity is being received (e.g., "I received 5 Dozen")
  2. **Price UOM** — the unit whose rate is used for pricing (e.g., "but the supplier quoted per Piece")

### Calculation logic:
```
qty_in_base          = quantity × purchase_uom.conversion_factor
price_per_base_unit  = price_uom_rate ÷ price_uom.conversion_factor
unit_price_original  = price_per_base_unit × purchase_uom.conversion_factor
line_gross_amount    = quantity × unit_price_original
```

### UOM auto-selection behavior (same as sales):
- When product is selected → both Purchase UOM and Price UOM auto-set to product's `is_default_sale_uom = true` UOM
- When Purchase UOM changes → Price UOM mirrors it automatically
- User can then manually override Price UOM independently

## 2. Discount System (3 levels — Purchase side accounting)

### Level 1: Trade Discount (per line item)
- Percentage (%) on gross amount
- **Never recorded in books** — only reduces the purchase price
- Net unit price = unit_price_original × (1 - trade_discount_pct / 100)
- Store `trade_discount_pct` and `trade_discount_amt` for reporting only
- **Does NOT enter Inventory cost** — Inventory is debited at net price after trade discount

### Level 2: Line Item Discount (per line item)
- Applied after trade discount, on net line amount
- Can be entered as percentage (%) or fixed amount (৳) — toggle between both
- **Reduces the inventory cost** — Inventory is debited at amount after this discount too
- Formula: `line_subtotal = line_net_amount - line_discount_amt`
- VAT (Input VAT) is calculated on `line_subtotal`

### Level 3: Bill Level Discount (on total bill)
- Applied on grand total after all line totals
- Accounting treatment:
  ```
  Dr. Accounts Payable             [amount]
      Cr. Purchase Discount A/C    [amount]  ← Contra Expense account
  ```
- Does NOT reduce Inventory cost — Inventory cost is already locked at line level
- Does NOT affect VAT — VAT is locked at line level

## 3. VAT & AIT — Purchase Side (Bangladesh context)

### Company Setting: VAT Registration Status
There must be a company setting: `is_vat_registered` (boolean).
This controls how Input VAT is treated:

#### If company IS VAT registered:
- Input VAT is a **recoverable asset** — recorded separately
- Inventory is debited at **net amount excluding VAT**
- Accounting entry per line item:
  ```
  Dr. Inventory / Stock A/C        [line_subtotal excluding VAT]
  Dr. Input VAT Receivable         [vat_amount]
      Cr. Accounts Payable             [line_subtotal + vat_amount]
  ```
- Input VAT Receivable is later offset against Output VAT Payable (from sales)

#### If company is NOT VAT registered:
- Input VAT becomes **part of the inventory cost**
- Accounting entry per line item:
  ```
  Dr. Inventory / Stock A/C        [line_subtotal including VAT]
      Cr. Accounts Payable             [line_subtotal + vat_amount]
  ```
- Weighted Average Cost is calculated including VAT in this case

### VAT modes per bill:
- **VAT Exclusive**: VAT added on top of line_subtotal → `vat_amount = line_subtotal × vat_rate`
- **VAT Inclusive**: VAT already in price → `vat_amount = line_subtotal × vat_rate ÷ (1 + vat_rate)`
- Mode selectable per bill (same as sales)
- Each line item uses its own VAT rate from product settings

### AIT (Advance Income Tax) — Purchase Side:
- AIT on purchase = amount **deducted from supplier payment** and deposited to NBR
- AIT is a **liability** (AIT Payable to NBR) — not an asset
- Accounting entry:
  ```
  Dr. Inventory / Stock A/C        [line_subtotal]
      Cr. Accounts Payable             [line_subtotal - ait_amount]
      Cr. AIT Payable (NBR)            [ait_amount]
  ```
- AIT rate per line item — auto-filled from product settings, editable

### Weighted Average Cost — how VAT affects it:
```
If VAT registered:
  net_unit_cost = line_subtotal_ex_vat ÷ qty_in_base_uom

If NOT VAT registered:
  net_unit_cost = (line_subtotal + vat_amount) ÷ qty_in_base_uom
```

## 4. Weighted Average Cost Update — Real-time on Purchase Save

Every time a purchase bill is saved, update `weighted_avg_cost` immediately:

```
new_avg_cost =
  (current_stock_in_base_uom × current_weighted_avg_cost) + (new_qty_in_base × new_net_unit_cost)
  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
                    current_stock_in_base_uom + new_qty_in_base_uom
```

Where `new_net_unit_cost`:
- If VAT registered: cost excluding VAT, after all discounts
- If NOT VAT registered: cost including VAT, after all discounts
- Always in base unit terms

After update:
- `products.weighted_avg_cost` = new_avg_cost
- `products.current_stock_in_base_uom` += new_qty_in_base_uom
- Insert row into `inventory_ledger`

## 5. Accounting Journal Entries — Auto-generated on Bill Save

### A. Purchase entries per line item (if VAT registered):
```
Dr. Inventory / Stock A/C        [line_subtotal excluding VAT]
Dr. Input VAT Receivable         [vat_amount]
    Cr. Accounts Payable             [line_subtotal + vat_amount - ait_amount]
    Cr. AIT Payable (NBR)            [ait_amount]  ← only if AIT applicable
```

### A. Purchase entries per line item (if NOT VAT registered):
```
Dr. Inventory / Stock A/C        [line_subtotal + vat_amount]
    Cr. Accounts Payable             [line_subtotal + vat_amount - ait_amount]
    Cr. AIT Payable (NBR)            [ait_amount]  ← only if AIT applicable
```

### B. Bill level discount entry (if applicable):
```
Dr. Accounts Payable             [bill_discount_amt]
    Cr. Purchase Discount A/C    [bill_discount_amt]
```

### C. Inventory ledger entry (perpetual system):
```
qty_in               = new_qty_in_base_uom
unit_cost            = new_net_unit_cost (at time of purchase)
total_cost           = qty_in × unit_cost
new_weighted_avg_cost = calculated above
reference_type       = 'purchase'
```

## 9. Important Business Rules

- Trade discount is **never posted to any account** on purchase side — it only reduces purchase price
- Line discount **reduces inventory cost** — Inventory is debited at net amount after line discount
- Bill level discount goes to **Purchase Discount (Contra Expense)** account — does not change inventory cost
- If company is VAT registered: **Input VAT is excluded from inventory cost** and recorded as asset
- If company is NOT VAT registered: **VAT is included in inventory cost**
- AIT on purchase is always a **liability** (AIT Payable to NBR) — never an asset on purchase side
- Weighted Average Cost update uses **net cost excluding VAT** if VAT registered, **including VAT** if not
- WAC snapshot (`weighted_avg_cost_before` and `weighted_avg_cost_after`) must be stored per line item for audit trail
- Bill level discount does **not** affect VAT — VAT already locked at line level
- A bill can only be edited if `payment_status = 'unpaid'`
- If a bill is deleted or reversed, **stock must be deducted** and **WAC must be recalculated**
